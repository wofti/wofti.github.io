Why it is stupid to buy a car like the Dodge Ram

I can see that you need a ram to properly storm a castle and smash through its gates. And since the castle defenders then will likely hurl rocks and missiles at you, you naturally want to dodge them, so a Dodge Ram seems like a good solution. Even though it is clear that there are even better vehicles these days (like tanks) for this kind of purpose, a Dodge Ram seems like an acceptable option for the average Joe. However, there is one fact that is totally overlooked in these considerations. How often do you actually have to storm castles these days? For me at least, it is so rare that I hardly remember the last time. And that is why buying a Dodge Ram (or similar car) is a stupid idea these days!!!

About the definition of Fascism

There are a lot of people these days who call others fascist. Prominent examples of people who are labeled fascist include Obama or the Orange Blob. However, I have the feeling that almost all the people how use this word, do not really know what fascism actually is. In fact the whole discussion really only makes sense once you have a clear definition of fascism. The best definition I have heard so far is this:

"Fascism may be defined as a form of political behavior marked by obsessive preoccupation with community decline, humiliation, or victim-hood and by compensatory cults of unity, energy, and purity, in which a mass-based party of committed nationalist militants, working in uneasy but effective collaboration with traditional elites, abandons democratic liberties and pursues with redemptive violence and without ethical or legal restraints goals of internal cleansing and external expansion."

This is from Robert Paxton's 2004 book (p.218). Paxton is a historian who has studied fascism for decades, and who published the above definition long before our current political divisions.

Therefore, just go through the points in the above definition to check how many apply to a particular movement or person. This way you can get a good idea who is a fascist, and who is not.
